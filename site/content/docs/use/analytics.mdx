## Overview

The Overview has six tiles: **Audience**, **Views**, **Content**, **Accounts**, **Comments** and **Likes**. Select a tile to see its detail below. The first time you open it, Twiligent loads recent posts for each account in the background; the numbers fill in after a short wait.

| Metric | How it is counted |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | YouTube subscribers plus Instagram followers. |
| Views | YouTube channel views, Instagram views where Instagram reports them, and views on tracked content. |
| Likes | YouTube likes and Instagram likes. |

## Account pages

Select an account in the sidebar to open its own page. YouTube channels have tabs for videos, engagement, publishing times, growth, Shorts and tags. Instagram pages show posting patterns, best days and hours, and top posts.

## Why a number looks wrong

- **Instagram views show a dash.** Instagram does not report views for some older posts or when insights are unavailable. A dash means "not reported", not zero.
- **Numbers are a little old.** Use **Refresh** on an account, or **Refresh all** in the top bar.
