## Publish a single post

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the type and file">

Open **Publish**, pick **Photo**, **Reel** or **Story**, then choose the file. Photos must be JPEG; Reels must be MP4 or MOV in 9:16; Stories accept either.

</Step>
<Step title="Write the caption and settings">

Write the caption (up to 2,200 characters). In the panel on the right, pick the account and optionally add alt text, collaborators, tagged people, a location, and for Reels a cover image and audio name.

</Step>
<Step title="Publish now or schedule">

Under **When**, keep **Publish now**, or pick **Schedule** and set a date and time. Then choose **Publish now** or **Schedule post**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you schedule

The file uploads straight away. The server publishes the post within 15 minutes after the time you picked, even when your browser is closed. If Instagram or the network has a passing problem, the server tries again on its next two runs. There is nothing for a member to set up.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for its time. You can edit the caption and time, or delete it. When it shows **Trying again on the next run**, the last attempt hit a passing problem and the Error column says which. |
| **Publishing** | The server is publishing it right now. It cannot be edited or deleted until it finishes. |
| **Published** | Live on Instagram. |
| **Failed** | Instagram rejected it, or three attempts in a row hit a problem. The Error column says why. Schedule the post again once the cause is fixed. |

<Callout type="warning" title="More than 30 minutes late and not yet published?">

A post still Pending 30 minutes after its time (or after its last attempt), or still Publishing 50 minutes after it started, means the scheduler on the server is not running. Email the administrator with the time of the post. Publish shows the same warning automatically.

</Callout>

## Bulk upload

Choose **Bulk upload** to add many files at once. Give each file its own caption or apply one caption to all, then either publish them one after another or **spread them out** from a start time at an interval such as once a day.

## Limits

- Instagram allows 100 posts per account in any 24 hours.
- Photos up to 8MB; videos up to 300MB and 15 minutes; Stories up to 60 seconds.
- Publishing needs Cloudinary set up by the administrator. If it is missing, Publish says so.
