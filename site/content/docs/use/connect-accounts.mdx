## YouTube

Open **Accounts**, choose **Add account**, then **YouTube channel**. Paste a channel link, an `@handle` or a channel ID starting with `UC`, choose **Find** to check it, then **Add channel**. Any public channel works; you do not need to own it.

## Instagram

Instagram only connects **Business** or **Creator** accounts. You can switch a personal account to a professional one for free in the Instagram app under Settings, Account type and tools.

### Get tester access first

Most instances run their Meta app in development mode. In that mode Instagram only lets accounts added as **testers** sign in. Without tester access, Instagram shows an error or a blank page.

<Steps>
<Step title="Ask the administrator">

Email your administrator with your Instagram username. The administrator adds you in the Meta app dashboard.

</Step>
<Step title="Accept the invite">

Sign in to Instagram on the web and open [Apps and websites](https://www.instagram.com/accounts/manage_access/), then the **Tester invites** tab, and accept the invite. In the Instagram app it is under Settings and privacy, Website permissions, Apps and websites, Tester invites.

</Step>
<Step title="Connect">

In Twiligent, open **Accounts**, choose **Add account**, then **Instagram account**, then **Continue to Instagram**. Approve every requested permission. Instagram sends you back and the account appears in the list.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Access lasts 60 days and renews itself">

Instagram access tokens last 60 days. Twiligent renews them automatically every night, so you only reconnect if you remove access in Instagram or change your password.

</Callout>

## Reddit

Reddit blocks most requests from servers, so Twiligent uses your browser session cookie to read your profile and posts.

1. Sign in to reddit.com in your browser.
2. Open the browser's developer tools (F12), then **Application**, then **Cookies**, then `https://www.reddit.com`.
3. Find the cookie named `reddit_session` and copy its value.
4. In Twiligent choose **Add account**, **Reddit account**, enter your username and paste the cookie.

<Callout type="warning" title="Treat the cookie like a password">

It signs in as you. Twiligent stores it on the server and never shows it again. If Reddit signs you out, the cookie stops working: remove the account and add it again with a fresh cookie.

</Callout>
