Automations send a private reply on Instagram when someone comments a keyword, such as GUIDE, on one of your posts or reels. A typical rule sends a link: "Here is your guide" and the address of the guide.

<Callout type="note" title="Before you start">

You need an Instagram Business or Creator account connected with Instagram sign-in, and the account must be public. Your administrator also turns on comment events for the server; until they do, the Automations page says comment events are not set up.

</Callout>

## Create a rule

1. Open **Automations** in the sidebar and choose **New rule**.
2. Choose the Instagram account, give the rule a name, and type its keywords, separated by commas.
3. Choose how keywords match. **Anywhere in the comment, as a whole word** answers "send me the guide please" for GUIDE but not "guidelines". **The whole comment, nothing else** answers only a comment that is the keyword.
4. Choose **Every post and reel**, or **Only the posts I choose** and pick them.
5. Write the reply and, if you like, a link. The preview shows the message as it will arrive, and how much of Instagram's 1,000 byte limit it uses.
6. Type a sample comment under **Test a comment** and choose **Test** to see whether it would match. Nothing is sent.
7. Leave **Turn the rule on when saved** on, and choose **Create rule**.

A comment gets one reply, from the first rule that matches it. Rules for chosen posts are checked before rules for every post. You can copy a rule (the copy starts off), change it, turn it off, or delete it.

## What Instagram allows

- One private reply per comment, sent within 7 days of the comment.
- The person can only get more messages from you after they reply.
- Up to 750 private replies an hour per account. Your administrator sets lower limits, and each rule can have its own.
- A person gets at most a few replies a day from one account, however many keywords they comment.

## Follow what happened

The counts at the top cover the chosen period. **Activity** lists every comment received on an account with an active rule, newest first, with its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Accepted by Instagram | Instagram took the reply. Instagram does not report whether the person read it |
| Waiting, Sending, Trying again | The reply is queued, going out, or waiting for a retry or a limit |
| Held | Replies for that account are paused |
| Needs review | The reply may or may not have gone out. Check the conversation on Instagram before you retry |
| Failed | Instagram refused it; the details say why |
| Skipped, Expired | The rule was turned off, the person reached the daily number of replies, or the 7 days ran out |
| No match | No rule answers this comment; its words are not kept |

Choose the retry button on a failed, skipped or review item to send it again. Instagram refuses a second private reply to the same comment, so a retry never messages someone twice.

## Pause an account

Turn off **Replies** next to an account to hold its replies, for example during a live event. Comments keep arriving and their replies go out when you turn it back on, if they are still within 7 days. If an account shows **Held**, Instagram refused its permission to send messages: connect it again on the **Accounts** page, then turn its replies back on.
