### Do I need GitHub to schedule posts?

No. Members never do. Administrators only need the deployed Worker; the GitHub workflow is an optional backup.

### Do I need my own Supabase project?

Only if you run your own instance. Members of someone else's instance use theirs.

### Why can't I connect my Instagram account?

It must be a Business or Creator account, and while the instance's Meta app is in development mode it must be added as a tester. See [Connect your accounts](/docs/use/connect-accounts).

### Can I connect a YouTube channel I do not own?

Yes. YouTube analytics use public data, so any public channel works.

### Does Twiligent publish to YouTube or Reddit?

No. Publishing is Instagram only. YouTube and Reddit are analytics only.

### What does running an instance cost?

For a small team, nothing. Supabase, Cloudflare, Cloudinary and Upstash free plans cover typical use, and the YouTube and Instagram APIs are free.

### How fresh is the data?

Data is fetched when you open a page or choose Refresh, and cached for a short time when Redis is set up. Instagram tokens are renewed nightly.

### Who do I contact for help?

The administrator of your instance: the person who gave you its link. The [Troubleshooting](/docs/use/troubleshooting) page lists what to tell them.
