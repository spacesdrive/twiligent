## What Twiligent does

Twiligent brings your YouTube channels, Instagram professional accounts and Reddit profiles into one place. You see their analytics side by side, publish and schedule Instagram posts, and follow individual Reddit posts and YouTube videos that matter to you.

Twiligent is self-hosted. Every copy of it, called an **instance**, belongs to the person or team who set it up. That person is the instance's **administrator**. Everyone who signs in to it is a **member**.

## What you can do

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Analytics** | Subscribers, followers, karma, views, likes and comments across platforms, with per-account detail pages. |
| **Libraries** | Every YouTube video and Short, every Instagram reel, post and video, and every Reddit post, searchable and filterable. |
| **Publishing** | Publish photos, Reels and Stories to Instagram now, schedule them for later, or upload many files at once. |
| **Tracked content** | Follow any Reddit post or YouTube video by link, group them into categories and compare them. |

<Callout type="note" title="Not sure whether you are a member or an administrator?">

If you only ever opened a link and signed in, you are a member: read [Using Twiligent](/docs/use/getting-started). If you created the Supabase project and deployed the app, you are the administrator: read [Run your own](/docs/self-host).
